summary: A client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.2 that remembers what is inside your /pv vaults, so you can see all of them at once without opening each one.
facts:
  - Opening a vault takes a snapshot of its contents, and the gallery shows one tile per vault as it was the last time you looked.
  - Search outlines every match in gold, including items packed inside shulker boxes up to 3 deep.
  - Everything happens on the client. The server is never sent anything it would not have received anyway.
