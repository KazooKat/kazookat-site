summary: Procedurally generates Minecraft spaceships and exports them as Litematica schematic files.
facts:
  - Pick a seed and a block palette, and get a ship you can paste into your world.
  - Hulls are segmented superellipse shapes with flat panels and chamfered corners, so parts line up by construction.
  - The pipeline goes seed, blueprint, 3D voxel shape, block roles, palette, then .litematic.
